
LEGAL AI KNOWLEDGE BASE

Document review:
AI systems can assist lawyers by identifying clauses,
extracting relevant information, and comparing contracts.

Contract analysis:
AI-assisted contract analysis can identify payment terms,
termination clauses, confidentiality obligations, and
potentially inconsistent provisions.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation:
RAG combines document retrieval with language generation.
A retriever identifies relevant document passages, while
a language model uses those passages to generate answers.

Human oversight:
AI-generated legal analysis should be reviewed by qualified
professionals before being used in legal decisions.

Confidentiality:
Legal AI systems must handle client information carefully.
Access controls, secure storage, and appropriate data
processing safeguards are important considerations.
